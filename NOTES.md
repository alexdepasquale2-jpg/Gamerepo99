E at a door (unless a talkable villager is closer) sleeps OverworldScene and launches `interior`; E at the exit mat/Esc wakes it at the door. Occupant = town's people sorted by id, one per building in layout order. Interior sets `body.dataset.hfLocalOverlay` so Esc leaves the room instead of opening the pause menu. QA hook: `window.__hearth.interior.{buildings(),enter(kind|buildingId|{townId,kind}),exit(),state()}` (instant, no fades). The overworld day clock/NPCs are frozen while inside; deeds are not available indoors.
